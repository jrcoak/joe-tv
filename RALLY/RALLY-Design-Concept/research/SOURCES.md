Additional aesthetic references named in the original brief included Apple, Netflix, Spotify, Stripe, Rivian, Neuron, Work & Co, GOATS, and Base31. RALLY adapts broad qualities—editorial restraint, clear hierarchy, confident motion, and product-specific craft—without reproducing any one interface.
